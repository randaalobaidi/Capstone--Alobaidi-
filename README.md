# Capstone--Alobaidi-
 project title Relationship between poverty and crime trends in the United States between 2016–2019 and 2021–2024
 objective  Evaluate whether poverty, as a Social Determinant of Health (SDoH), is associated with violent and property crime rates at the state level
 current status/next step Examining the relationship between poverty and crime trends in the United States from 2016–2019 and 2021–2024. I integrated state-level crime data with American Community Survey (ACS) poverty estimates to construct a multi-year panel dataset. After cleaning and merging the datasets, I calculated crime rates per 100,000 population to ensure comparability across states.
